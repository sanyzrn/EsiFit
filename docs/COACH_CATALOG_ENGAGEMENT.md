# Coach workspace, catalog and install promotion — 25 Oct 2026

## Decisions and verified sources
- PWA promotion follows [web.dev's guidance](https://web.dev/articles/promote-install): capture the browser event globally, keep native installation user-initiated, wait for visible engagement, remember dismissal and avoid critical journeys. EsiFit's chosen thresholds (not a browser standard) are 90 visible seconds plus two distinct routes or three trusted interactions, at most once per session, and a seven-day cooldown. Previous permanent dismissals remain respected. Standalone/appinstalled suppress promotion. Auth/live-workout/AI routes, focused inputs and open dialogs suppress the card. The approved BrandLogo replaces the obsolete dumbbell.
- [ACSM's 2026 position stand](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/) emphasizes regular participation, all major muscle groups at least twice weekly and individualized load/volume. Eight editable starting points cover two/three-day full-body, four/five-day upper/lower, three-day legacy split, home, dumbbell and advanced six-day PPL. These are starting points, not universal medical prescriptions. Existing named templates are preserved; new PPL generation uses a real seven-day cycle with three training days. Stored legacy plans are not rewritten.
- [USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/) is the source for 46 added foods. `usda-foods.ts` retains exact FDC IDs, English record/state and source URLs. Nutrients per 100g are scaled to declared grams (including practical oil/salt/sugar servings). Raw/cooked/salted states remain explicit. Existing 94 approximate recipes/foods retain their recorded values; no false claim of clinical precision.
- RepDB's current [650-entry catalog and license](https://github.com/RepDB/exercise-dataset) were reviewed at `b407c5951a027a27817fb2a5cee9ec9feffc4dc7`. See EXERCISE_MEDIA_LICENSES.md. The canonical Persian library now has 100 selected movements. 96 have exact equipment/movement matches and four use labeled independent first-party vector schematics; no inaccurate lookalike substitution, premium previews or AI-derived RepDB artwork.

## Coach behavior
- Roster search normalizes Persian/Arabic keyboard forms; filters identify athletes needing attention or lacking a plan. Active-program count is scoped to the visible active roster.
- Athlete records show the actual active prescription, profile targets/equipment, recent readiness, pain, goals, weight, volume and seven days of recorded nutrition. Unlogged days are unknown, not zero intake. Nutrition totals share the diary calculation.
- ProgramEditor copies an existing plan or one of eight presets. Coaches edit a seven-day cycle, exercise order, rest days, sets, repetition range, RPE, rest and per-movement notes; bounded one-to-eight-week blocks repeat explicit prescriptions without hidden automatic progression. Equipment mismatch blocks saving. Legacy shorter cycles are padded only in the unsaved copy. Name/goal/athlete-facing description remain editable.
- Review/confirmation states that the plan starts today and replaces the current active plan. The existing User and relationship rows serialize writes; roles and active ownership are rechecked in the transaction. Optimistic source-plan IDs reject stale edits. Old plans/sessions remain; creation, weekly target and athlete notification commit atomically. Assignment uses the same existing daily quota as legacy/AI assignment.
- Independent private relationship notes support empty-string clearing. Coaches cannot edit other coaches' athletes; administrators get the supervision view without a falsely enabled coach action.
- This is not a claim of a complete coaching CRM: messaging, booking, billing, medical rehabilitation, arbitrary nutrition prescriptions and future program scheduling are outside this revision.
